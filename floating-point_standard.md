## The official Coolinggrooling online floating point standard .TM

## Constructor

There are two constructors for floating point numbers

- 1.
- -1.

There is no other way to create a float

## Operations

There are 4 operations on floats

### add

increment float by 1.

#### Examples

1.++ = 2.
-1.++ = 0.

### Subtract

decrement float by -1.

#### Examples subtract

1.-- = 0.
-1.-- = -2.

### Decimal right shift

Shifts the decimal point to the right

#### more examples

1.>> = 10.
-1. >> -10.

### Decimal left shift

Shifts the decimal point to the left

#### Extra examples

1.<< = .1
-1.<< = -.1
1.<<<< = .01
1.<<>> = 1.
